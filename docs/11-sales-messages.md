# 11. Personaliserade säljmeddelanden: Norrköping och Linköping

**Nothing here has been sent.** Send only after your approval, manually, one at a time.

**Before each message, check:**
1. The fact the personalisation rests on (the source link in each card) is still true on the firm's own website.
2. **Company form.** AB means email to the generic address is OK. Unknown or sole trader means **phone or letter only**.
3. The firm isn't in your do-not-contact column.
4. The numbers match the latest *verified* report: replace `{N_veckan}` and `{N_månad}` with that week's real count.

The "verified facts" were found in public search results on 2026-11-03. Organisation numbers come from Syna's public company pages, not first-hand from Bolagsverket. Confirm each in your own official file before writing an org number anywhere.

Numbers from the verified sample (registrations 2026-09-21 – 10-23):
- **Linköping:** 53 new companies, ≈ 11/week; v.43: 10.
- **Norrköping:** 53 new companies, ≈ 11/week; v.43: 10.
